Cutoff Guide

Check the next cutoff and aid station on your Garmin.

Cutoff Guide uses race-specific cutoff and aid station data to help runners check what comes next during a race.

What it shows

  • Next cutoff point
  • Distance to cutoff
  • Cutoff time
  • Time left
  • Next aid station
  • Distance to aid station

Who it is for

  • Runners worried about cutoff times
  • Runners aiming to finish within the time limit
  • Ultramarathon runners
  • Runners who want aid station distances on their Garmin
