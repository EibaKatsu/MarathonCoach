Cutoff Guide

Know your next cutoff before it becomes a problem.

Cutoff Guide shows the next cutoff point, time left, and distance to the next aid station on your Garmin watch. It is built for races where checking your phone or calculating cutoff times in your head is not realistic.

View Supported RacesRequest a Race on X

What you can check during the race

  • Where the next cutoff is
  • How far away the cutoff is
  • How much time is left before the cutoff
  • Where the next aid station is
  • How far away the next aid station is

Who it is for

  • Runners in races with many cutoff points
  • Runners trying to finish while managing the time limit
  • Ultramarathon or overseas race runners who want cutoff and aid awareness
  • Runners who do not want to check a phone during the race
Screen Fields
  • Row 1: Distance of the next cutoff point and the remaining distance to it.
  • Row 2: Configured cutoff time and the remaining time before it.
  • Row 3: Distance to the next aid station and the remaining distance.
Race-specific

Race-specific by design

Cutoff Guide is delivered race by race. Cutoff times, aid stations, wave starts, and course changes vary by event, so always check the supported race pages for the race you plan to run.
