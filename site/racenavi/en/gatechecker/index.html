Cutoff Guide

Check cutoff times and aid stations on your Garmin.

Cutoff Guide is a Garmin app for runners who want to check cutoff points, cutoff times, remaining time, and aid stations during a race. Install one app, enter the Race Code for your race in Garmin Connect, and the app will show the cutoff and aid station data for that race and course.

It is built for races where checking your phone or calculating cutoff times in your head is not realistic.

What you can check during the race

  • Where the next cutoff is
  • What time the next cutoff is
  • How far away the cutoff is
  • How much time is left before the cutoff
  • How far away the next aid station is

One app. Many races.

Cutoff Guide does not use separate apps for each race. Find your Race Code on this website, enter it in the app settings, and sync your watch.

  • Install Cutoff Guide from Connect IQ
  • Find your Race Code on this website
  • Enter the Race Code in Garmin Connect app settings
  • Sync your watch
  • Check cutoff and aid information during the race
Screen Fields
  • Row 1: Check the distance and cutoff time of the next cutoff point.
  • Row 2: Check the remaining distance and time to the next cutoff point.
  • Row 3: Check the remaining distance to the next aid station.
