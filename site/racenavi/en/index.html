RaceNavi

Heart rate and pacing

RaceNavi helps you check your heart rate, pace, target gap, and estimated finish time during a marathon.

Information

  • Current heart rate
  • Heart-rate cap
  • Current pace
  • Gap from target
  • Estimated finish time

Cutoff Guide

Cutoff times and aid stations

Cutoff Guide helps you check the next cutoff point and aid station on your Garmin watch.

Information

  • Next cutoff point
  • Distance to cutoff
  • Cutoff time
  • Time left
  • Next aid station
  • Distance to aid station
