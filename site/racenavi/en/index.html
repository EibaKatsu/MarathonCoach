Apps

The problem changes depending on the race.

Some days the real question is pace control. On other days it is just whether you are safely getting to the next cutoff. That is why these are split into two apps.

RaceNavi

If you are chasing a goal time, you want to know when you are pushing too hard or letting it slip.

RaceNavi puts heart rate, pace, gap to goal, and predicted finish on one screen. It is for the part of the race where you want to judge early overcooking and late fade without piecing the numbers together in your head.

Cutoff Guide

If cutoffs are what keep bothering you, look at the next cutoff first.

Even if you studied the cutoff table before the race, the small details disappear once you are moving. Cutoff Guide shows the remaining distance and remaining time to the next cutoff on Garmin. Aid info is there when you need it, but it is secondary.

It works as one app plus a Race Code for each race and course. Some races are free samples, and you can also request a race that is not listed yet.
