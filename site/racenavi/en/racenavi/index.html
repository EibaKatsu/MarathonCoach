RaceNavi

Heart rate and pacing on one Garmin screen.

RaceNavi helps you check your current heart rate, pace, target gap, and estimated finish time during a marathon.

What it shows

  • Current heart rate
  • Heart-rate cap
  • Current pace
  • Gap from target
  • Estimated finish time
  • Distance
  • Elapsed time

Who it is for

  • Runners who want to avoid going out too hard
  • Runners who use heart rate during races
  • Runners aiming for a target finish time
  • Runners who want a simple Garmin race-day screen
