Custom Setup

Tune RaceNavi a bit closer to your own race.

Two runners chasing the same finish time can still have very different heart-rate behavior and late-race fade. This page is for runners who want to adjust RaceNavi more closely to their own race and past data.

Send a monitor request on XSee what is needed

Who this is for

  • You want the upper effort guide tightened a bit for your goal pace
  • You often fade late and want a better plan for the first half
  • You have Garmin or FIT data but are not sure how to use it
  • You want RaceNavi adjusted around a specific goal race

What I would need

  • Target finish time
  • Max heart rate
  • Resting heart rate
  • LTHR if available
  • Recent race data or FIT data
  • Your target race
