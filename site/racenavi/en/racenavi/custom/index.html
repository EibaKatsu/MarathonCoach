Custom Setup

Tune RaceNavi for your own race.

Custom Setup helps prepare RaceNavi settings based on your target time, heart-rate profile, and past running data.

What is included

  • Target time review
  • Heart-rate profile review
  • Heart-rate cap suggestions
  • Race-day pacing notes
  • RaceNavi custom code, if available

Information needed

  • Target time
  • Max heart rate
  • Resting heart rate
  • LTHR if available
  • Recent race or FIT data
  • Target race
