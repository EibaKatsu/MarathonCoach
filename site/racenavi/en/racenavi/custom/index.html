Custom Setup

Tune RaceNavi for your goal race.

Runners with the same finish goal still respond differently in heart rate and late-race fade. Custom Setup is meant to organize RaceNavi settings around your target time, heart-rate profile, and past race data.

Send a monitor request on XSee Required Info

Who this is for

  • Runners who want a clearer upper effort guide for their target pace
  • Runners who often fade late and want a better early-race plan
  • Runners who have Garmin or FIT data but are not sure how to use it
  • Runners who want RaceNavi tuned for a specific goal race

Required information

  • Target finish time
  • Max heart rate
  • Resting heart rate
  • LTHR if available
  • Recent race data or FIT data
  • Your target race
